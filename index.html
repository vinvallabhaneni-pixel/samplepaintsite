A family name Westchester has trusted since the Carter administration

Since 1979, Korth & Shannahan has handled the interior painting, exterior painting, and finish carpentry for homes across Westchester County. Will Korth and his team have built the business one referral at a time, the same reason so many clients call us back for every project since.

Every job is staffed by our own trained, full-time crew, never day labor. That consistency is how we hit deadlines without cutting corners, and why our work earned us the title of Best General Contractor from Westchester Magazine.
