Transformative home painting and carpentry in Westchester County

Custom millwork, painting, molding, cabinetry, and more, handled by one full-time crew who follows every job from planning through cleanup.

Residential Painting Services

We've always been the premier interior and exterior residential painting company homeowners trust throughout our history. We've painted hundreds of homes throughout Westchester County. Our team can help you choose the perfect color to match your home's style and your own personal aesthetic, whether you want bold, beautiful colors or something calming and understated.

  • Interior: vibrant, high-quality paints to bring your room design to life
  • Exterior: paint products designed to withstand New York's weather while boosting curb appeal

Carpentry Services

Our trusted master carpenters can handle any custom millwork or carpentry project, backed by our four-year warranty. Finish carpentry work can improve both your home's energy efficiency and its style.

  • Window & door installation
  • Custom millwork and cabinetry installation
  • Crown molding & baseboards
  • Deck repairs and replacement carpentry

Historical Restoration

If you live in a historic home, you're required by state and local ordinances to maintain a certain style when updating it. Since 1979, we've provided historic home restoration for homeowners throughout the community, with licensed employees who understand the importance of your home's unique charm.

  • Exterior painting to period-accurate standards
  • Crown molding, baseboard & beam wraps
  • Historic paint and color consultations

Lead Paint Removal

Lead paint was used in homes until the late 1970s, and is especially hazardous for young children. If your home was built before 1970, there's a strong chance it was painted with lead paint. Our knowledgeable and trained team has the equipment and certification to remove it safely.

Deck Staining & Restoration

We revitalize decks that have faded due to weather, with restoration for rotting boards, handrails, and beams, finished with a fresh stain so you get the most from your outdoor entertainment space.

Wallpaper Install & Removal

Wallpaper is a laborious, time-consuming project best left to professionals. We offer both careful removal of existing paper and clean, professional-looking installation.
